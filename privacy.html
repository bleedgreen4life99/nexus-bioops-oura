Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026

NEXUS BioOps is a personal, noncommercial integration intended for use by its owner.

Data requested

With authorization, the integration may request Oura sleep, readiness, activity, heart-rate, and other metrics only to the extent permitted by the scopes granted and the available Oura API endpoints.

Purpose and storage

The intended use is to create private health-data records for the owner's personal analysis. The planned implementation stores exports in the owner's chosen iCloud Drive location. The public website does not upload or store Oura health records.

Authorization

Oura authorization is optional and may be revoked through Oura's account controls. Access tokens and authorization codes are sensitive credentials and must not be posted in public repositories, screenshots, or support requests.

Sharing

The project is not intended to sell or share Oura health records with advertisers or other users. Data may be processed by Oura, Apple/iCloud, and other services the owner explicitly chooses to use under their own terms.

Website hosting

This public informational site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may collect technical request information under GitHub's privacy practices.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration's actual handling of data changes. The owner should review it before using the integration beyond personal testing.
